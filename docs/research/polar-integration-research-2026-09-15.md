# Polar billing fit — 2026-09-15

**Decision status:** research only. BlogFactory Cloud pricing, checkout, entitlements, limits, cancellation, and support policy remain unshipped; billing must stay outside MCP and server-side entitlements must be authoritative ([`FEATURE_PLAN.md`](../../FEATURE_PLAN.md)). Sources below are official Polar docs, checked today.

## What Polar supports

- **Catalog and checkout.** A product is one-time or recurring (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly); pricing can be fixed, pay-what-you-want, free, metered, or seat-based. Metered charges can sit beside a fixed base fee and multiple meters can attach to one product. Product billing cycle/pricing type are immutable after creation, and existing subscribers retain their price. Checkout Sessions take a product-ID array plus server-set customer, trial, seat/unit, discount, metadata, and success/return fields. [Products](https://polar.sh/docs/features/products) · [Checkout API](https://polar.sh/docs/api-reference/checkouts/create-session)
- **Subscriptions and teams.** Recurring checkout creates the subscription and first order; renewal charges the saved payment method and failed renewal becomes `past_due`. Customers normally have one active subscription per organization unless multiple subscriptions are enabled. Seat pricing supports subscription or one-time products, but is explicitly **beta**; its buyer is distinct from seat holders and benefits are granted only when a seat is claimed. [Subscriptions](https://polar.sh/docs/features/subscriptions/introduction) · [Seats](https://polar.sh/docs/features/seat-based-pricing)
- **Usage.** Polar ingests immutable events, filters/aggregates them into meters, and bills metered subscription products per cycle. Current documented metered model is linear unit pricing; volume pricing is marked coming soon. A backdated event is charged in the cycle when Polar receives it, never retroactively. [Usage billing](https://polar.sh/docs/features/usage-based-billing/billing) · [Event ingestion](https://polar.sh/docs/features/usage-based-billing/event-ingestion)
- **Credits.** A Credits benefit can add units to a meter once on a one-time purchase, or every subscription cycle; rollover is configurable. A credits-only meter avoids usage overage billing. This is meter credit, not a general-purpose cash wallet. [Credits](https://polar.sh/docs/features/usage-based-billing/credits)
- **Customer experience and entitlement inputs.** Hosted checkout, customer portal, invoices/receipts, self-service cancellation and payment-method updates are provided. Benefits (including feature flags and credits) are automated, but should be projected into BlogFactory’s own server-side entitlement record. The hosted portal is intentionally unbranded; custom portal APIs do not cover payment-method updates. [Customer portal](https://polar.sh/docs/features/customer-portal/introduction) · [Portal settings](https://polar.sh/docs/features/customer-portal/settings) · [Feature flags](https://polar.sh/docs/features/benefits/feature-flags)
- **Webhooks.** Signed webhook delivery has retries (up to 10), a 10-second timeout, and disables an endpoint after 10 consecutive non-2xx results; Polar recommends a fast acknowledgement and async processing. Process `customer.state_changed`, subscription, order, refund, and benefit events idempotently and tolerate re-delivery/out-of-order delivery. [Delivery](https://polar.sh/docs/integrate/webhooks/delivery) · [Events](https://polar.sh/docs/integrate/webhooks/events)
- **Merchant of Record.** Polar says it acts as reseller and assumes international sales-tax liability/collection/remittance; BlogFactory still owes its own income/revenue taxes. Polar supports partial/full refunds, may proactively refund within 60 days to reduce chargebacks, and a subscription-order refund does not itself end the subscription. [MoR](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/introduction) · [Refunds](https://polar.sh/docs/features/refunds) · [Fees and disputes](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/fees)

## Limits that matter to BlogFactory

- Polar **never blocks action when a meter/credit balance is exceeded**. Its balance is useful for billing/customer display, not a safe authorization or spend gate. Events are immutable, so replay/error correction needs a deliberate local accounting path. [Event ingestion](https://polar.sh/docs/features/usage-based-billing/event-ingestion)
- Polar is a higher-level MoR, so it expressly trades some advanced billing-model flexibility/control for its abstraction and tax handling. Metered volume pricing is not yet available; seat pricing is beta. Do not assume unsupported mixed carts, custom wallet transfers, credit-refund mechanics, or an API idempotency guarantee: prove the exact API version/flow in sandbox. The public docs do not expose a numeric API rate limit; handle `429` with backoff. [MoR trade-offs](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/introduction) · [Sandbox](https://polar.sh/docs/integrate/sandbox)

## Recommendation: Cloud plan + optional AI Credits

1. Use Polar for the external commercial layer: server-created checkout, customer mapping via `external_customer_id`, hosted portal, signed webhooks, and an idempotent local entitlement projection. Never use a browser success redirect as payment proof.
2. Keep BlogFactory as the source of truth for tenant/site authorization, quotas, concurrency, spend caps, and any AI-credit balance. Append an immutable local usage/credit ledger; atomically reserve/debit it before provider work, then send Polar usage only after the committed billable event. This protects against retries, late events, insufficient funds, and refunds.
3. **Yes, sell prepaid “BlogFactory AI Credits” alongside BYO AI—but only as an optional managed-AI wallet.** Sell a one-time credit product that grants a normalized BlogFactory unit (not raw “tokens” unless one fixed provider/model/rate card defines it). Customers using BYO AI should consume their own provider account and receive no credit debit; the Cloud plan can coexist with either mode. Polar Credits can mirror/package the purchase, but must not be the runtime ledger or the authority that enables generation.
4. Do not launch credit packs until product, finance/tax, refund/chargeback, provider-cost/rate-card, expiry/rollover, and support decisions define what a credit buys and how a refunded purchase reverses local access/balance. Validate checkout composition and one-time-credit/subscription coexistence in Polar sandbox rather than infer it from the products-array API.
